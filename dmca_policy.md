# DMCA Policy

Davidnet is a personal project operated independently from the Netherlands. We respect the intellectual property rights of others and expect our users to do the same.

## Reporting Copyright Infringement
If you believe that content hosted on Davidnet infringes your copyright, you can submit a DMCA takedown notice. To be effective, your notice must include the following information:

* An electronic or physical signature of the person authorized to act on behalf of the owner of the copyright interest.
* A description of the copyrighted work that you claim has been infringed.
* A description of where the material that you claim is infringing is located on Davidnet (please provide direct URLs).
* Your contact information, including your address, telephone number, and email address.
* A statement by you that you have a good faith belief that the disputed use is not authorized by the copyright owner, its agent, or the law.
* A statement by you, made under penalty of perjury, that the above information in your notice is accurate and that you are the copyright owner or authorized to act on the copyright owner's behalf.

## Submitting Your Notice
You can send your copyright infringement notices or questions to us via our [Contact Page](https://davidnet.net/legal/contact) or directly through our ticket system at [https://davidnet.net/help/tickets](https://davidnet.net/help/tickets).

You can review all other legal policies and platform rules at [https://davidnet.net/legal](https://davidnet.net/legal).
