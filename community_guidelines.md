# Community Guidelines

Davidnet is a personal project operated independently from the Netherlands, and we want to maintain a positive, welcoming environment for everyone. By participating in our community, you agree to follow these guidelines.

## Be Respectful
* **Treat others with courtesy:** Healthy discussions and disagreements happen, but personal attacks, harassment, and bullying will not be tolerated.
* **Keep it civil:** Avoid hate speech, discrimination, or malicious provocation directed at any individual or group.

## Keep Content Clean & Safe
* Do not post sexually explicit content, graphic violence, self-harm material, or illegal content. 
* Respect other users' privacy and do not share private information without consent.

## Reporting & Moderation
If you encounter content or behavior that violates these guidelines, please use the report button available on public content. You can track the status of your reports at [https://davidnet.net/moderation/reports](https://davidnet.net/moderation/reports).

Failure to follow these guidelines may result in content removal, account restrictions, or termination. If you need to contact us directly, please visit our [Contact Page](https://davidnet.net/legal/contact).

You can review all other legal policies and platform guidelines at [https://davidnet.net/legal](https://davidnet.net/legal).
