# Acceptable Use Policy

Davidnet is a personal project operated independently from the Netherlands. By accessing or using our platform, you agree to follow this Acceptable Use Policy.

## Prohibited Activities
You agree not to use Davidnet to engage in any activity that is illegal, harmful, or abusive. This includes, but is not limited to:

* **Unlawful Conduct:** Violating any applicable local, national, or international laws or regulations (including EU and Dutch laws).
* **Security Breaches:** Attempting to bypass, probe, scan, or test the vulnerability of Davidnet's systems, or interfering with any user, host, or network (e.g., distributing malware, launching DDoS attacks).
* **Abuse & Harassment:** Harassing, threatening, or bullying other users, or distributing hate speech, explicit content, or spam.
* **Intellectual Property Violations:** Uploading, sharing, or distributing content that infringes upon the intellectual property or privacy rights of others.
* **Unauthorized Access:** Attempting to gain unauthorized access to other user accounts, tokens, or restricted areas of the platform.

## Reporting Content
If you find content that violates these guidelines, you can use the report button available on any public content. You can view the status of your reports and track them here: [https://davidnet.net/moderation/reports](https://davidnet.net/moderation/reports).

## Enforcement
Violations of this Acceptable Use Policy may result in the suspension or termination of your account, removal of content, or other appropriate action. If you need to reach out to us directly, please use our [Contact Page](https://davidnet.net/legal/contact).

You can review all other legal policies and platform guidelines at [https://davidnet.net/legal](https://davidnet.net/legal).
