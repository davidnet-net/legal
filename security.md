# Security

Davidnet is a personal project operated independently from the Netherlands, and we take the security of our platform and user data seriously. 

## Reporting Vulnerabilities
If you discover a security vulnerability or bug on Davidnet, we appreciate your help in disclosing it responsibly. Please do not publicly share or exploit the vulnerability. Instead, report it to us immediately so we can investigate and resolve it via our [Contact Page](https://davidnet.net/legal/contact).

## Infrastructure Security
To protect user accounts, tokens, and platform traffic, Davidnet relies on secure edge infrastructure. For details regarding our third-party infrastructure and hosting partners, please see our [Sub-Processors Policy](https://davidnet.net/legal/sub_processors).

You can review all other legal policies and platform guidelines at [https://davidnet.net/legal](https://davidnet.net/legal).
